CONSTRAINED

God, consciousness, freedom, and the human condition.

Twenty-four chapters. Each one keeps a fact, what follows from it, and a line it will not cross.

The gold marks on the cover jump to six places to start. This page is not a store. The book is not for sale here yet.

Six places to start

The cover holds them. The sentences are the same ones the chapters use.

Chapter 1 · The Form

The form

A birth certificate records a birth. It can matter to an office. It is not a second person, a trust, or a bank account.

Not claimed: that every office handles records the same way, or that records never get misused.

Chapter 2 · Words With Consequences

The words

A promise, a warning, or a contract can do real work. That depends on the situation and who has authority to hear it. A private password, or a trick of capitalization, does not.

Not claimed: that this is settled law, or that every contract is fair.

Chapter 4 · The Asset Called Tuesday

The day

A schedule, care, or debt can claim your Tuesday. The day itself is not a piece of property someone owns.

Not claimed: that an average Tuesday is your Tuesday.

Chapter 13 · The Galilean Question

The look-alike

Two stories can look alike. That is not proof one was copied from the other. A look-alike that fails the sources is not proof the other story is true.

Not claimed: that comparing stories is useless, or that the question of contact is closed.

Chapters 16–17

The year 325

Christians used exalted language for Jesus before the council of 325. That council drew a public line inside a fight already underway. It did not invent the devotion from nothing.

Not claimed: that early devotion is the later creed, or that the creed is therefore true.

Chapters 6 and 19

The timing

A lab can catch a brain signal before a person says they chose a small movement. That is a lab result. It is not the sentence “the brain decided,” and it does not prove or disprove a Creator.

Not claimed: that this settles free will, or that experience is unreal.
