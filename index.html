A form can make a birth legible. It can't contain a person.

A birth record does a limited job. It can make a birth legible to institutions. It cannot contain a person.

Fact: registration systems produce evidence.

Inference: that evidence has real administrative effects.

Speculation we refuse: the record is a hidden person, trust, or financial instrument.

Rejecting an unsupported myth is not a defense of every bureaucracy.
