A birth certificate records a birth. It is not the person.

The form tells an office that a birth happened. That is the job. It does not hold the person, and it does not create the person.

  • Fact: a birth record is evidence that a birth was registered.
  • What follows: offices act on that record.
  • Not claimed: the record is a hidden person, a trust, or money.

Calling that myth false is not the same as saying every office is right.
